Spotify (Un)Wrapped

How the page works

View What every graph is about, from genres down to tracks. Each view keeps its own pins and hidden picks.

Timeline Drag the window to move through your history, or drag an edge to resize.

‹›

Period Select preset window sizes, pan between periods, and look into specific years.

Pin Click an artist anywhere (a band, chip or tile) to keep them lit and in every graph. Click again to unpin.

Search Pin anyone in your history, even outside your top list.

Show and hide Clicking a chip's color tab hides that artist everywhere.

Colors Pick the artists' color scheme, or toggle between light and dark.

No export yet?

Only totals are shared: hours, play counts, your top 500 artists and tracks with hours, and your weekday × hour listening grid. No timestamps, IP addresses, devices or locations. You can preview it and remove it any time.

How do I get my data?
  1. Go to Spotify → Privacy settings and scroll to Download your data.
  2. Check Extended streaming history and click Request data.
  3. Spotify emails you a zip (can take a few days). Drop it above.
